Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

At DivineNest ("we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize the privacy and security of our visitors and community members. This Privacy Policy outlines the information we collect when you visit divinenest.com, watch embedded YouTube videos, submit contact or music forms, or subscribe to our newsletter.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, and phone number when submitting inquiries, collaboration requests, or music proposals. We also collect anonymous aggregate telemetry (such as page views and browser types) to improve website speed and accessibility.

3. Embedded YouTube Videos & Cookies

Our website embeds video content hosted on YouTube. When you interact with embedded YouTube players, YouTube/Google may set cookies and collect usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not store or sell your viewing habits to third parties.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our "DivineNest Kids" section is designed to be completely family-friendly and safe. We do not knowingly collect personal contact information from children under 13 years of age. All forms are intended for parents, guardians, and adult creators.

5. Your Rights & Inquiries

You have the right to request access to or deletion of your contact information and newsletter subscription at any time by emailing us at contact@divinenest.com.
